✳ Before you hit record

Good questions.
Straight answers.

no fine-print gymnastics.
Do I need a big following?+

No follower minimum. You need a connected social account and content that meets the campaign brief. A clip starts earning once it reaches 1,000 verified views, subject to the campaign’s rules.

What kind of content can I make?+

Whatever fits the brief: a music edit, a GRWM, a product demo, a funny skit, or a slice of everyday life. Each campaign tells you what to say, show, tag, and avoid. You create and edit with your own tools.

How are my earnings calculated?+

The brand sets a rate per 1,000 verified views. Only full thousands pay: at ₦500 per 1,000 views, 4,900 views earns ₦2,000. Your clip must be approved, and earnings are limited by the remaining campaign budget and clip or creator caps.

When can I withdraw my money?+

Earnings have a five-day holding period so suspicious views can be checked. Once cleared and payable, you can request a withdrawal to a Nigerian bank account. The product targets delivery within 24 hours of becoming payable; bank processing may vary.

What if my clip gets rejected?+

You get the specific checklist items your content did not meet, with reasons. If you think the decision is wrong, you can appeal. You should always know what happened and what you can do next.

What happens when the campaign budget runs out?+

The campaign closes when the pot is exhausted or its deadline passes. It cannot pay more than it holds. Check the remaining budget and the campaign caps before creating; example calculator results are not reserved earnings.

Is Clippit available outside Nigeria?+

The first version is built for creators in Nigeria, with naira payouts to Nigerian bank accounts. Platform availability depends on supported account connections. International payouts are not part of the first release.
